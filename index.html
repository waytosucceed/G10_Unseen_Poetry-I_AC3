Key Takeaways


  1. Who is the speaker of a poem?
    We know that the speaker is the voice that speaks inside a poem, and it is not necessarily the poet who wrote it.

  2. What is a persona?
    A persona is an invented character through whom a poet speaks, like a mask the poet puts on.

  3. How should we refer to the voice of a poem when writing about it?
    We know that we should always refer to "the speaker", and use the poet's name only when discussing the poet's choices.

  4. What is the difference between a confessional voice and an observational voice?
    A confessional voice looks inward and reveals the speaker's own feelings, while an observational voice looks outward and describes a scene, other people or events.

  5. What is an unreliable speaker?
    We know that an unreliable speaker is one whose account or judgement cannot be fully trusted, because they are mistaken, biased, deceiving themselves or hiding something.

  6. Why do poets use unreliable speakers?
    Poets use unreliable speakers to create a gap between what the speaker believes and what the reader can see, and the poem's real meaning often lies in that gap.

  7. What is a volta?
    We know that a volta is the point where a poem changes direction in its thought, argument, mood or tone.


Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Rue
Meaning: To feel deep regret or remorse about something.
Example: He would later rue the decision to dismiss the warning signs so casually.

Tendance
Meaning: A tendency, trend, or prevailing inclination, especially in behaviour, thought, or fashion.
Example: The novel reflects the growing tendance among writers to challenge traditional social norms.

Trot
Meaning: A moderately fast pace of movement, especially of a horse, between a walk and a gallop.
Example: The rider guided the horse into a steady trot along the forest trail.
